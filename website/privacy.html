// PRIVACY POLICY

Your vault stays
on your device.

Effective 2026-10-25 · CofreTabs 1.1.x · Mad Wolf Studios

CofreTabs is a local-only tab vault. It has no backend, no user accounts, no analytics and no cloud sync. Saved tabs, folders and notes live in IndexedDB inside your browser profile.

1. What CofreTabs stores

When you explicitly press SAVE THIS TAB, CofreTabs stores the page title, full URL, hostname, automatic folder, timestamps, an inline favicon only if the browser supplies a local data: image, any note or folder changes you make, and a trash state if you move the entry to Trash.

It does not store browsing history, open-tab lists, cookies, form data, clipboard contents or passwords. It does not read pages you have not chosen to save.

2. What CofreTabs does not do

  • No account, sign-in, email address or API key
  • No analytics, telemetry, crash reporting, ads or tracking pixels
  • No remote scripts, fonts, styles or favicon requests
  • No upload of saved pages, titles, URLs, notes or backups
  • No reading of history, cookies or clipboard contents
  • No execution of saved text as HTML or JavaScript

3. Permissions

activeTab lets the extension read the current tab's title and URL only after you click the toolbar icon. scripting lets it inject the save panel after that same click. CofreTabs requests no host permissions and no always-on content scripts.

Browser-internal pages such as chrome://, the Chrome Web Store and PDF viewers cannot be injected by any extension. CofreTabs opens its library with an explanation instead; it does not try to bypass browser restrictions.

4. Backups

Exported backups are JSON files created locally and downloaded through your browser. Where they go is controlled by you. CofreTabs never uploads them. Backups contain URLs, titles and notes and are not encrypted — treat them as personal data.

5. Favicons and network requests

Only inline data:image/* favicons supplied by the browser are stored. CofreTabs does not fetch remote favicon URLs and makes no runtime network requests. Its stylesheet and Public Suffix List are bundled with the extension.

6. Website folders

Automatic folders are derived locally from a bundled snapshot of the ICANN section of the Public Suffix List. Hostnames are not sent to a lookup service. Private PSL rules are excluded, so shared platforms group by platform.

7. Other software

Your browser, operating system, backup software and any installed store may process browser-profile data under their own policies. CofreTabs does not control those products. A store may collect install/update telemetry independently of the extension.

8. Your control

Trash hides entries without deleting them. Delete forever and Empty Trash remove records from IndexedDB. Deleting a folder moves its active tabs to Trash. Export a backup before clearing browser data or uninstalling. Uninstalling or clearing extension data removes the local vault according to browser behaviour. There is no server-side copy to delete because none is ever created.

9. Sensitive data

Do not use CofreTabs as a password manager, health record store or other sensitive-data vault. Saved URLs, titles and notes are stored unencrypted in your browser profile. Use full-disk encryption and OS account security.

10. Changes and contact

Material changes will be documented in the changelog and release notes, and the effective date will be updated. Questions: madwolfstudios.com.

CofreTabs is open source under the MIT license. Verify these claims in the source: no remote fetch in src/, two permissions in manifest.json, and a single database layer in src/storage/vault.js.
